## Salmon Curry Olivia

1.  Using a sharp knife, remove the skin from

    * [ ] 3-4 pounds of salmon

    Cut it into 1"-by-2" chunks.

2.  Prepare vegetables (each one optional, but the more the better):

    * [ ] 2 carrots, peeled and cut into 3/4-inch chunks
    * [ ] small potatoes, cut into 1-inch pieces if not tiny enough
    * [ ] 1/2 head of cabbage or half a napa cabbage, cut into 1-inch squares
    * [ ] 1 zucchini, cut in half and sliced into 3/4-inch chunks
    * [ ] 1 large red onion, peeled and cut longitudinally into 1/4 to 1/2 inch slices
    * [ ] 1 red bell pepper, seeded, cut into 1-1/2" to 2" chunks
    * [ ] 1 eggplant, cut in half lengthwise, then each half cut into thirds crosswise, and cut each sixth into wedges about 1 inch wide at the widest point

    Start rice!

3.  Skim the fat layer off the top of a can of

    * [ ] 1 14.5-oz can of coconut cream

    Heat the fat over medium in a large dutch oven. Add

    * [ ] 1 small can curry paste

    Stir until fragrant, 1-2 minutes.

    Increase to medium-high heat. Add

    * [ ] the potatoes and carrots

    Cook, stirring, for 2 minutes. Then add

    * [ ] the cabbage

    Cook, stirring, until the cabbage starts to soften, about 5-8 minutes. Then add

    * [ ] the zucchini

    Cook 1 minute. Now add:

    * [ ] the rest of the can of coconut cream
    * [ ] 2 additional 14.5-oz cans of coconut milk
    * [ ] the eggplant

    Turn the heat to high and bring to a boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer 20 minutes.

4.  Add:

    * [ ] the salmon
    * [ ] the bell pepper

    Boil for 2 minutes, then add

    * [ ] the onion

    Cook until the salmon is done, we think about 3 minutes more.
