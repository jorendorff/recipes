# Chickpeas tikka masala

*Chickpeas + rice is a complete protein, but we agreed what this recipe is missing is chicken thighs.*

### Prep

Dice:

* [ ] 2 medium yellow onions

Mince, into a single bowl:

* [ ] 8 cloves garlic
* [ ] 2 tbsp ginger
* [ ] 2 green chilis

Mix in a bowl:

* [ ] 2 tsp Kashmiri chili powder (or 1 tsp paprika and 1/2 tsp cayenne)
* [ ] 2 tsp cumin
* [ ] 2 tsp coriander
* [ ] 1 tsp turmeric
* [ ] 1 tsp paprika


### Cook

1.  In a large Dutch oven, heat until shimmering:
    * [ ] 6 tbsp ghee

    Add:
    * [ ] the onions

    Cook, stirring occasionally, until the edges of the onions are golden and the onions are soft, 7-8 minutes.

2.  Add:
    * [ ] the garlic, ginger, and chili

    Cook, stirring, until fragrant, about a minute. Don't let the garlic brown.

3.  Add:
    * [ ] the spices

    Stir to coat. Cook until toasty and slightly darkened, about 30 seconds.

4.  Add:
    * [ ] 6 tbsp tomato paste

    Stir constantly, pressing the tomato paste into the pan. Cook until it darkens from bright red to a deeper brick red and the ghee begins to separate around the edges of the pan, 3-4 minutes.

    This, the recipe says, is the key step.

5.  Add:
    * [ ] 2 tsp kosher salt
    * [ ] 2 tsp sugar
    * [ ] 1 1/2 cups water

    Stir well, scraping up any bits on the bottom of the pan. Bring to a simmer and cook 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens slightly and the onion is very soft.

6.  Stir in:
    * [ ] 2 tsp garam masala
    * [ ] 1 cup heavy cream

    Return to a gentle simmer. Do not boil.

7.  Add:
    * [ ] 4 15-oz cans of chickpeas (or other protein already cooked)

    Stir to coat. Simmer medium-low for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. The sauce should coat the back of a spoon. If it's too thin, reduce; if too thick, add water.

8.  Crush with your hands directly into the pot:
    * [ ] 4 tsp dried kasoori methi leaves (fenugreek)

    Simmer 2 minutes to bloom.

    Taste and adjust salt.

Serve hot on rice. Drizzle cream on top to be fancy.
