# Black beans

*This recipe can make a soup, but it's best as a thickened bean dish served over rice.*

Make

* rice

Wash

* 1 lb black beans

And put them in a saucepan, covered with water. Add

* 4 tsp salt

Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for two hours, until soft enough you can remove a bean and easily mash it with a fork.

Meanwhile, chop

* celery
* carrots
* ham
* whatever

and saute in

* oil or butter

Once that's cooked, shove it aside in the pan, and in the remaining oil bloom the spices (these amounts are guesses):

* 2 tbsp cumin
* 1 tbsp paprika
* 2 tsp crushed dried oregano
* 2 tsp crushed dried thyme

Then stir the spices into the vegetable mix and remove from heat.

Once the beans are cooked, adjust the water level. If they're wetter than you like, drain some water. If they're too dry, add:

* broth (or water)

Scoop out 1 cup of beans, mash them thoroughly, and return them to the pot. The risk of burning the bottom of your beans is now extreme, so stay with them and stir occasionally.

Add in the vegetables. Salt to taste. Serve on

* rice
