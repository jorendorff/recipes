# Turkey sausage

Prepare the seasoning first.

## Seasoning for herb sausage

Toast in a pan:

* [ ] 1/4 tsp cumin seeds

Then saute:

* [ ] 3 cloves garlic

At the end, add:

* [ ] 1/4 tsp smoked paprika

Put all of the above in a small bowl with:

* [ ] 1 tsp salt
* [ ] 1/4 tsp dried thyme
* [ ] 1/4 tsp dried oregano
* [ ] 1/8 tsp dried minced onions
* [ ] 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
* [ ] 3 tbsp fresh sage
* [ ] 1-2 tbsp fresh parsley


## Seasoning for hot sausage

Saute:

* [ ] 5 cloves garlic

Transfer to a small bowl and add:

* [ ] 1 tsp salt
* [ ] 1/2 tsp dried rosemary (or 2-3 tsp fresh)
* [ ] 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
* [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
* [ ] 1/4 tsp dried minced onions


## The home stretch

Shred:

* [ ] 1/2 medium zucchini

and squeeze it (in cheesecloth if you've got it) to remove as much moisture as possible.

In a large bowl, combine the following ingredients. **Caution:** Stir very carefully. Don't overmix anything with ground turkey; it gets tough.

* [ ] 1 lb ground turkey
* [ ] the shredded zucchini
* [ ] 3 1/3 oz. queso blanco
* [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
* [ ] the seasoning from above

Form into patties and fry over moderate heat. The sausage should be sizzling happily but not too energetically. (Low heat is also important to keep the turkey from getting tough.)
