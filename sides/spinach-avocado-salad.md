# Spinach avocado salad with lemonade vinaigrette

*The taste of summer.*

https://urbanfarmie.com/spinach-avocado-salad/#wprm-recipe-container-3636

*Doubled, and I added a lot of sugar to make the vinaigrette palatable.*

## Lemonade vinaigrette

Whisk together:

- [ ] juice of 2 lemons
- [ ] 1/4 cup sugar

Dissolve the sugar. Then whisk in:

- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 3/8 cup olive oil

## Salad

Serve with:

- [ ] 12 oz baby spinach
- [ ] 1 cup chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 1 or 2 large ripe avocados, sliced
- [ ] 1/2 cup cojita cheese
- [ ] thinly sliced radishes

Make sure to use enough dressing.
