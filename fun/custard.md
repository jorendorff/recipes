# Vanilla custard

*This recipe is deliberately a little low on the cornstarch, to make a pourable custard. We ate it with chocolate cake and strawberries. It was amazing.*

In a large saucepan, stir together:

* [ ] 3 cups whole milk or half and half
* [ ] 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
* [ ] 3/4 cup sugar
* [ ] 1/4 tsp plus a pinch of salt

Warm over medium heat, stirring frequently, just til the edges bubble.

Remove from heat, cover, and let steep 15 minutes.

In a separate bowl, whisk together

* [ ] 6 egg yolks
* [ ] 3 tbsp cornstarch

until very smooth.

Slowly ladle about a cup of the hot milk mixture into the yolks, whisking constantly to temper them.

Then pour the yolk mixture into the saucepan, again whisking constantly.

Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly with a silicone spatula, until it thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, 5-7 minutes. Do not boil.

Remove from heat. Add, a tablespoon at a time,

* [ ] 3 tbsp butter

Mix until smooth and glossy. Then add

* [ ] 1 1/2 tbsp vanilla paste

The original recipe says to push this through a fine-mesh sieve. As to whether this is necessary, use your judgment.
